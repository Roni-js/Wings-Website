# Our Team — Gallery photos

Drop **vertical / portrait team photos** here.

- File names: `1.jpg`, `2.jpg` … `20.jpg`
- **Horizontal (landscape) photos.** Recommended ratio **3:2** or **16:9**
  (e.g. 1600 × 1067 px). They are cropped to fill, so keep faces centred.
- The gallery shows **5 photos at a time** and slides forward automatically.
- No names are shown — photos only.
- Supported: `.jpg` `.jpeg` `.png` `.webp` `.avif`
